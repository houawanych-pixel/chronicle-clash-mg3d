# Provenance
Animation clips are based on Mixamo animations (Adobe), used under Adobe's Mixamo terms. Characters made and rigged with Tripo AI.

This project contains no Metal Gear source code, and no character sprite sheets or proprietary game assets from Metal Gear are included. Meshes are blockout art, not finished character assets.

Synthesized sound cues are reused from Chronicle Clash MG Build 01. DejaVu Sans fonts are included under the license reproduced in DejaVu-fonts.txt.
